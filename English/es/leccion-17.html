Lección 17 · The Spirit is Moving Nivel A1 · Principiante

Presente continuo en negativo y preguntas (isn't / aren't + verb-ing)

Vocabulario nuevo: 12 · Acumulado: 205 / 1200
1. Watch · Objetivos y resumen

🎯 Objetivo principal

Aprender a usar el presente continuo en sus formas negativa y interrogativa (preguntas), para describir acciones que NO están ocurriendo o preguntar si están ocurriendo ahora mismo.

📌 Subobjetivos

  • Formar el negativo del presente continuo: am not / isn't / aren't + verb-ing.
  • Formar preguntas en presente continuo: Am I / Is he / Are you + verb-ing?
  • Usar respuestas cortas: Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
  • Comprender el versículo “The Spirit of God was moving over the waters” (Génesis 1:2) adaptado al presente continuo: “The Spirit is moving over the waters.”
En esta lección aprenderás a usar el presente continuo en negativo y en preguntas. Verás cómo se forma el negativo: I am not moving, you are not moving, he is not moving, etc. También aprenderás a hacer preguntas: Am I moving?, Are you moving?, Is he moving?, y a responder con respuestas cortas. Practicarás con el versículo "The Spirit of God was moving over the waters" de Génesis 1:2, adaptado al presente. Al final, podrás preguntar y negar acciones en progreso.
In this lesson, you will learn to use the present continuous in negative and questions. You will see how the negative is formed: I am not moving, you are not moving, he is not moving, etc. You will also learn to ask questions: Am I moving?, Are you moving?, Is he moving?, and to answer with short answers. You will practice with the verse "The Spirit of God was moving over the waters" from Genesis 1:2, adapted to the present. By the end, you will be able to ask and deny actions in progress.
Observa: En “The Spirit is moving over the waters”, usamos el presente continuo para hablar de una acción en progreso. En esta lección, aprenderás a decir “The Spirit is not moving” y a preguntar “Is the Spirit moving?”

6. Grammar · Presente continuo: negativo y preguntas

🔹 Negativo del presente continuo

Para hacer el negativo, añadimos not después del auxiliar am/is/are:

Sujeto + am/is/are + not + verb-ing
PronombreNegativo (forma larga)Negativo (contraído)Ejemplo
Iam not moving—I am not moving.
Youare not movingaren't movingYou aren't moving.
Heis not movingisn't movingHe isn't moving.
Sheis not movingisn't movingShe isn't moving.
Itis not movingisn't movingIt isn't moving.
Weare not movingaren't movingWe aren't moving.
Theyare not movingaren't movingThey aren't moving.

🔹 Preguntas en presente continuo

Para hacer preguntas, invertimos el sujeto y el auxiliar am/is/are:

Am/Is/Are + sujeto + verb-ing?
PreguntaRespuesta corta
Am I moving?Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
Are you moving?Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
Is he moving?Yes, he is. / No, he isn't.
Is she moving?Yes, she is. / No, she isn't.
Is it moving?Yes, it is. / No, it isn't.
Are we moving?Yes, we are. / No, we aren't.
Are they moving?Yes, they are. / No, they aren't.
Reglas clave:
  • Negativo: am not / isn't / aren't + verb-ing
  • Pregunta: Am/Is/Are + sujeto + verb-ing?
  • Respuestas cortas: Yes, + pronombre + am/is/are / No, + pronombre + am not/isn't/aren't
En contexto bíblico: En Génesis 1:2, el Espíritu se movía sobre las aguas. Usando el presente continuo en pregunta: “Is the Spirit moving over the waters?” Y en negativo: “The Spirit is not moving.”
