Lección 22 · They Were Afraid Nivel A1 · Principiante

Pasado de "to be" en negativo (wasn't / weren't) y preguntas (Was...? Were...?)

Vocabulario nuevo: 12 · Acumulado: 267 / 1200
1. Watch · Objetivos y resumen

🎯 Objetivo principal

Aprender a usar el pasado de "to be" en su forma negativa (wasn't / weren't) y interrogativa (Was...? Were...?) para preguntar y negar sobre el pasado, con respuestas cortas.

📌 Subobjetivos

  • Formar el negativo: wasn't (I, he, she, it) y weren't (you, we, they).
  • Formar preguntas: Was (I, he, she, it)? y Were (you, we, they)?
  • Responder con respuestas cortas: Yes, I was. / No, I wasn't.
  • Comprender el versículo “They were all afraid” (Marcos 4:41).
En esta lección aprenderás a usar el pasado de to be en negativo y preguntas. El negativo se forma con wasn't y weren't. Las preguntas se forman con Was y Were al principio. Practicarás con la historia de los discípulos en la tormenta, cuando Jesús calmó el viento y las olas, y ellos tuvieron miedo. Al final, podrás preguntar y negar sobre el pasado usando was/were correctamente.
In this lesson, you will learn to use the past of to be in negative and questions. The negative is formed with wasn't and weren't. Questions are formed with Was and Were at the beginning. You will practice with the story of the disciples in the storm, when Jesus calmed the wind and the waves, and they were afraid. By the end, you will be able to ask and deny about the past using was/were correctly.
Observa: En el versículo “They were all afraid” (afirmativo). En esta lección aprenderás a decir “They weren't afraid” y a preguntar “Were they afraid?”

6. Grammar · Negativo y preguntas con was/were

🔹 Negativo del pasado de "to be"

Para negar en pasado, añadimos not después de was o were. Las contracciones son wasn't y weren't.

PronombreNegativoContracciónEjemplo
Iwas notwasn'tI wasn't afraid.
Youwere notweren'tYou weren't there.
Hewas notwasn'tHe wasn't at home.
Shewas notwasn'tShe wasn't a nurse.
Itwas notwasn'tIt wasn't a good day.
Wewere notweren'tWe weren't happy.
Theywere notweren'tThey weren't in the garden.

🔹 Preguntas en pasado con was/were

Para hacer preguntas, invertimos el sujeto y el verbo: Was/Were + sujeto + complemento?

PreguntaRespuesta corta
Was I afraid?Yes, I was. / No, I wasn't.
Were you afraid?Yes, I was. / No, I wasn't.
Was he afraid?Yes, he was. / No, he wasn't.
Was she afraid?Yes, she was. / No, she wasn't.
Was it a good day?Yes, it was. / No, it wasn't.
Were we happy?Yes, we were. / No, we weren't.
Were they afraid?Yes, they were. / No, they weren't.
Reglas clave:
  • Negativo: wasn't (I/he/she/it) / weren't (you/we/they)
  • Pregunta: Was (I/he/she/it) / Were (you/we/they) + sujeto + complemento?
  • Respuestas cortas: Yes, + pronombre + was/were. / No, + pronombre + wasn't/weren't.
En contexto bíblico: En Marcos 4:41, los discípulos were muy asustados. Usando el negativo, podemos decir que “They weren't calm” (no estaban calmados). Y la pregunta “Were they afraid?” tiene una respuesta afirmativa.
