Lección 31 · Jesus Said, "Come, Follow Me" Nivel A1 · Principiante

Pasado simple de verbos irregulares: say → said, go → went, come → came, leave → left

Vocabulario nuevo: 12 · Acumulado: 379 / 1200
1. Watch · Objetivos y resumen

🎯 Objetivo principal

Aprender a usar los verbos irregulares más comunes en pasado simple: say → said, go → went, come → came, leave → left, y practicar otros irregulares como see → saw, do → did, have → had.

📌 Subobjetivos

  • Memorizar la forma en pasado de verbos irregulares comunes.
  • Diferenciar entre verbos regulares (-ed) e irregulares (cambio de forma).
  • Usar verbos irregulares en pasado para contar historias bíblicas.
  • Comprender el versículo “Jesus said, 'Come, follow me.' And they went” (Marcos 1:17-18).
En esta lección aprenderás los verbos irregulares más importantes en pasado. A diferencia de los verbos regulares que añaden -ed, los irregulares cambian de forma: say → said, go → went, come → came, leave → left. Verás cómo se usan para contar la historia de Jesús llamando a sus primeros discípulos. Al final, podrás narrar historias bíblicas usando verbos irregulares con confianza.
In this lesson, you will learn the most important irregular verbs in the past. Unlike regular verbs that add -ed, irregular verbs change their form: say → said, go → went, come → came, leave → left. You will see how they are used to tell the story of Jesus calling his first disciples. By the end, you will be able to tell Bible stories using irregular verbs with confidence.
Observa: En “Jesus said, 'Come, follow me.' And they went”, los verbos said y went son formas irregulares en pasado. No añaden -ed.

“Jesus said, 'Come, follow me.' And they went.” — Marcos 1:17-18 (EasyEnglish Bible)

Lectura extendida (Marcos 1:16-20):

“As Jesus walked beside the Sea of Galilee, he saw Simon and his brother Andrew. They were fishermen. Jesus said, 'Come, follow me. I will make you fishers of people.' At once, they left their nets and went with him. He went a little farther and saw James and John, the sons of Zebedee. They were in a boat, mending their nets. Jesus called them. Immediately they left their father Zebedee in the boat with the hired men and followed him.”

— Marcos 1:16-20 (EASY)

Palabras clave para escuchar (verbos irregulares):

  • said – /sed/ – dijo
  • went – /wɛnt/ – fue / caminó
  • came – /keɪm/ – vino
  • left – /lɛft/ – dejó
  • saw – /sɔː/ – vio
  • called – /kɔːld/ – llamó (regular)

Traducción: “Mientras Jesús caminaba junto al mar de Galilea, vio a Simón y a su hermano Andrés. Eran pescadores. Jesús dijo: 'Venid, seguidme. Os haré pescadores de hombres.' Al instante, dejaron sus redes y lo siguieron.”

6. Grammar · Verbos irregulares en pasado

Pasado (irregular)

I said.

I went.

I came.

I left.

Reglas clave:
  • Irregulares: la forma en pasado cambia completamente.
  • No se añade -ed a los verbos irregulares.
  • Hay que memorizar las formas en pasado.
  • En negativo y preguntas con did, el verbo vuelve a su forma base: I didn't go (no went).
En contexto bíblico: En Marcos 1:17-18, “Jesus said, 'Come, follow me.' And they went.” Said y went son verbos irregulares esenciales para contar la historia del llamado de Jesús.
