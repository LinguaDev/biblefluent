Lección 54 · As Far as the East is from the West Nivel A2 · Pre-intermedio

Comparativos de igualdad: as... as y not as... as

Vocabulario nuevo: 12 · Acumulado: 662 / 1200
1. Watch · Objetivos y resumen

🎯 Objetivo principal

Aprender a usar los comparativos de igualdad (as... as) para decir que dos cosas son iguales en alguna cualidad, y los comparativos de desigualdad (not as... as) para decir que no son iguales.

📌 Subobjetivos

  • Usar as + adjetivo + as para expresar igualdad.
  • Usar not as + adjetivo + as para expresar desigualdad.
  • Usar as much/many + sustantivo + as para cantidades.
  • Comprender el versículo “As far as the east is from the west, so far has he removed our transgressions from us” (Salmo 103:12).
En esta lección aprenderás a usar as... as para comparar igualdades. La estructura es: as + adjetivo + as. Por ejemplo: "He is as tall as his brother." También aprenderás not as... as para decir que algo no es igual: "She is not as patient as her sister." Practicarás con el versículo de Salmo 103:12: "As far as the east is from the west, so far has he removed our transgressions from us." El perdón de Dios es tan inmenso como la distancia entre el oriente y el occidente.
In this lesson, you will learn to use as... as to compare equalities. The structure is: as + adjective + as. For example: "He is as tall as his brother." You will also learn not as... as to say that something is not equal: "She is not as patient as her sister." You will practice with the verse from Psalm 103:12: "As far as the east is from the west, so far has he removed our transgressions from us." God's forgiveness is as immense as the distance between the east and the west.
Observa: En “As far as the east is from the west, so far has he removed our transgressions from us”, se usa as... as para comparar la distancia entre el oriente y el occidente con la inmensidad del perdón de Dios.

6. Grammar · Comparativos de igualdad

🔹 Comparativos de igualdad: as... as

Los comparativos de igualdad se usan para decir que dos cosas o personas son iguales en alguna cualidad.

as + adjetivo + as
Igualdad (as... as)

He is as tall as his brother.

She is as patient as her mother.

This book is as interesting as that one.

Desigualdad (not as... as)

He is not as tall as his brother.

She is not as patient as her mother.

This book is not as interesting as that one.

🔹 Con sustantivos: as much / as many

as much + (sustantivo incontable) + as · as many + (sustantivo contable) + as
  • I have as much faith as you.
  • They have as many children as we do.
as... as (igualdad)

as strong as

as beautiful as

as wise as

not as... as (desigualdad)

not as strong as

not as beautiful as

not as wise as

Reglas clave:
  • as + adjetivo + as = igualdad (tan... como).
  • not as + adjetivo + as = desigualdad (no tan... como).
  • El adjetivo va en forma base (sin -er ni more).
  • Para cantidades: as much as (incontable) y as many as (contable).
En contexto bíblico: El salmista usa “As far as the east is from the west” para comparar la inmensidad del perdón de Dios. Es una imagen de distancia infinita.
