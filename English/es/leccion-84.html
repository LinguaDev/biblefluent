Lección 84 · He Asked If I Believed Nivel A2 · Unidad 7

Discurso indirecto para preguntas (if, whether y WH-words) · Juan 9:35-38

Vocabulario nuevo: 8 · Acumulado: 1020 / 1200
1. Watch · Objetivos y resumen

🎯 Objetivo principal

Aprender a reportar preguntas usando el discurso indirecto con if, whether y palabras WH-. Entenderemos el diálogo de Jesús con el ciego de nacimiento en Juan 9:35-38: “Do you believe in the Son of God?” → “He asked if I believed in the Son of God.”

📌 Subobjetivos

  • Comprender la estructura de las preguntas indirectas con if y whether.
  • Reconocer el backshifting (cambio de tiempos) en preguntas indirectas.
  • Practicar la transformación de preguntas directas a indirectas.
En esta lección aprenderemos a reportar preguntas. Cuando reportamos una pregunta que se responde con sí/no, usamos "if" o "whether". Ejemplo: "Do you believe?" → He asked if I believed. Cuando la pregunta tiene una palabra WH- (who, what, where, etc.), usamos esa palabra para conectar. Ejemplo: "Who is he?" → He asked who he was. Los tiempos verbales retroceden (presente → pasado, futuro → condicional). Practicaremos con el diálogo de Jesús y el ciego de nacimiento.
In this lesson we will learn to report questions. When we report a yes/no question, we use "if" or "whether". Example: "Do you believe?" → He asked if I believed. When the question has a WH-word (who, what, where, etc.), we use that word to connect. Example: "Who is he?" → He asked who he was. Verb tenses shift back (present → past, future → conditional). We will practice with the dialogue between Jesus and the blind man.
Observa: En “He asked if I believed”, el verbo believe cambia de presente a pasado (believed) al reportar la pregunta.

(Directo) “Do you believe in the Son of God?”
(Indirecto) “He asked if I believed in the Son of God.” — Juan 9:35

Lectura extendida (Juan 9:35-38, ESV):

“Jesus heard that they had cast him out, and having found him he said, 'Do you believe in the Son of Man?' He answered, 'And who is he, sir, that I may believe in him?' Jesus said to him, 'You have seen him, and it is he who is speaking to you.' He said, 'Lord, I believe,' and he worshiped him.”

Estructuras de preguntas indirectas:

  • Yes/No questions: “Do you believe?” → He asked if I believed. / He asked whether I believed.
  • WH- questions: “Who is he?” → He asked who he was.
  • WH- object: “What did you see?” → He asked what I had seen.
  • WH- place: “Where do you live?” → He asked where I lived.

Más ejemplos bíblicos:

  • He asked, “Who touched me?” → He asked who had touched him. (Marcos 5:30)
  • She asked, “Where have you put him?” → She asked where they had put him. (Juan 20:13)
  • They asked, “Are you the King of the Jews?” → They asked whether he was the King of the Jews. (Mateo 27:11)

Traducción: “Jesús oyó que lo habían expulsado, y cuando lo encontró, le dijo: '¿Crees en el Hijo del Hombre?' Él respondió: '¿Y quién es, Señor, para que crea en él?' Jesús le dijo: 'Ya lo has visto; es el que está hablando contigo.' Entonces él dijo: 'Creo, Señor', y lo adoró.”

    6. Grammar · Preguntas indirectas

    🔹 Discurso indirecto para preguntas

    Yes/No questions

    “Do you believe?” → He asked if I believed.

    “Are you a Christian?” → He asked whether I was a Christian.

    “Have you been saved?” → He asked if I had been saved.

    WH- questions

    “Who is he?” → He asked who he was.

    “What did you see?” → He asked what I had seen.

    “Where do you live?” → He asked where I lived.

    “When will you come?” → He asked when I would come.

    Regla clave:
    • Para preguntas de sí/no, usa if o whether.
    • Para preguntas con WH-, usa la misma palabra WH-.
    • Los tiempos verbales retroceden (backshift): presente → pasado, pasado → pasado perfecto, futuro → condicional.
    • El orden de las palabras cambia: sujeto + verbo (no inversión como en la pregunta directa).
