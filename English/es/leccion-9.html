Lección 9 · We Have This Hope Nivel A1 · Principiante

Negativo y preguntas con el verbo have (don't have / doesn't have / Do you have?)

Vocabulario nuevo: 12 · Acumulado: 105 / 1200
1. Watch · Objetivos y resumen

🎯 Objetivo principal

Aprender a usar el verbo have en negativo (don't have / doesn't have) y en preguntas (Do you have? / Does he have?) para hablar de posesiones y carencias.

📌 Subobjetivos

  • Formar negativas con don't have (I/you/we/they) y doesn't have (he/she/it).
  • Formar preguntas con Do you have? y Does he/she have?
  • Responder preguntas con respuestas cortas: Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
  • Comprender el versículo “We have this hope as an anchor for the soul” (Hebreos 6:19).
En esta lección aprenderás a usar el verbo have en negativo y en preguntas. Verás cómo se forma el negativo: I don't have, you don't have, he doesn't have, she doesn't have, it doesn't have, we don't have, they don't have. También aprenderás a hacer preguntas: Do I have?, Do you have?, Does he have?, Does she have?, Does it have?, Do we have?, Do they have? Practicarás con el versículo "We have this hope as an anchor for the soul" de Hebreos 6:19. Al final, podrás preguntar y negar posesiones correctamente.
In this lesson, you will learn to use the verb have in negative and questions. You will see how the negative is formed: I don't have, you don't have, he doesn't have, she doesn't have, it doesn't have, we don't have, they don't have. You will also learn to ask questions: Do I have?, Do you have?, Does he have?, Does she have?, Does it have?, Do we have?, Do they have? You will practice with the verse "We have this hope as an anchor for the soul" from Hebrews 6:19. By the end, you will be able to ask and deny possessions correctly.
Observa: En el versículo “We have this hope” se usa la forma afirmativa. En esta lección aprenderás a decir “We don't have hope” y a preguntar “Do you have hope?”

6. Grammar · Negativo y preguntas con "have"

🔹 Negativo con el verbo "have"

Para formar el negativo de have en presente, usamos el auxiliar do/does + not + have:

PronombreNegativoContracciónEjemplo
Ido not havedon't haveI don't have a car.
Youdo not havedon't haveYou don't have money.
Hedoes not havedoesn't haveHe doesn't have a dog.
Shedoes not havedoesn't haveShe doesn't have a sister.
Itdoes not havedoesn't haveIt doesn't have a tail.
Wedo not havedon't haveWe don't have time.
Theydo not havedon't haveThey don't have money.

🔹 Preguntas con el verbo "have"

Para hacer preguntas, usamos Do / Does al principio + sujeto + have:

PreguntaRespuesta corta
Do I have a car?Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
Do you have money?Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
Does he have a dog?Yes, he does. / No, he doesn't.
Does she have a sister?Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't.
Does it have a tail?Yes, it does. / No, it doesn't.
Do we have time?Yes, we do. / No, we don't.
Do they have money?Yes, they do. / No, they don't.
Reglas clave:
  • I / You / We / They → don't have (negativo) / Do + sujeto + have? (pregunta)
  • He / She / It → doesn't have (negativo) / Does + sujeto + have? (pregunta)
  • ¡Cuidado! En negativo y pregunta, have vuelve a su forma base (no se usa has).
En contexto bíblico: En Hebreos 6:19, la afirmación “We have this hope” se convierte en negativo: “We don't have this hope” (no tenemos esta esperanza) o en pregunta: “Do you have this hope?” (¿Tienes esta esperanza?).
